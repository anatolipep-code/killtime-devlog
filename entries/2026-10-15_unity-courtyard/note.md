# The courtyard in Unity: first in-engine frames

- Claude Code (the coding agent) rebuilt the Blender test courtyard in Unity 6 URP from the megablock kit, entirely from code: the scene, materials and VFX are generated by editor scripts.
- What is on screen: wet ground with neon reflections (planar reflections on the strong profile, probes on the weak one), VFX Graph rain with splashes and gallery drips, height fog with fake light shafts, about 20 lights with two broken flickering lamps, a glitchy hologram billboard, a procedural skyline.
- Two quality profiles switch live: "Target" for an RTX 3060 and "Laptop" for an integrated Intel Arc with 0.5 GB VRAM. On that laptop: 140+ fps on Laptop, 110+ on Target at 1600x900.
- Honest status: some shots are too dark (good mood, bad for spotting enemies), the vertical neon signs read as black boxes from the main angles (kit fix already pushed), and a few rain streaks look like lasers. No gameplay yet.
- Screenshots are straight from the built player, made by the game's own capture mode.
