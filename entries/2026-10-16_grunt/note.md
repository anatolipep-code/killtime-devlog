# First enemy: the Grunt

- An armed debtor of the megablock courtyard: hooded rain jacket, respirator with red goggles, a scrap-metal chest plate, a red armband (the community's mark), a taped-up scrap rifle.
- He has a SPINE on his left wrist too: that is what the player grabs in the finisher to drain his time.
- 3,064 triangles with the rifle, 23-bone rig with Unity Humanoid names, rigid low-poly skinning like the player's arms.
- 11 animation clips, all generated by code and solved with IK so both hands stay on the rifle: idle/aim, two walks, run, fire, reload (a visible window for the player), hit, two deaths, and "Grabbed" - the victim being yanked by the wrist, convulsing while his time drains, then collapsing.
- Honest status: procedural animation, not motion capture. Readable and stiff-ish; good enough to build the AI and the fight on, will get polish passes.
- Made by Claude (AI) from scripts in Blender; renders are Blender Cycles, not in-engine yet.
