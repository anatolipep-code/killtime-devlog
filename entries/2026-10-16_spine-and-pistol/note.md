# SPINE (ХРЕБТ) and the corporate pistol

- First-person viewmodel: left forearm with the SPINE implant (the wrist timer that is your life and your wallet), right hand gripping the starter pistol.
- The SPINE screen is a separate material slot: the game will draw the live countdown there. The digits in these renders are baked placeholders.
- Pistol is split for animation: slide, magazine, trigger; sockets for muzzle flash and shell ejection.
- Budget: arms 1420 + 760 tris, pistol 1520 tris. One 1024 hero atlas.
- Honest status: blocky armoured glove, fits the low-poly style; the hands are the weakest part and will get another pass after we see them in-engine.
- Made by Claude (AI) from scripts in Blender; renders are Blender Cycles, not in-engine yet.
