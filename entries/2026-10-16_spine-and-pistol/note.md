# SPINE (ХРЕБТ) and the corporate pistol

- First-person viewmodel: left forearm with the SPINE implant (the wrist timer that is your life and your wallet), right hand gripping the starter pistol.
- The SPINE screen is a separate material slot: the game will draw the live countdown there. The digits in these renders are baked placeholders.
- Pistol is split for animation: slide, magazine, trigger; sockets for muzzle flash and shell ejection.
- Version 1 of the grip was a robot claw: fingers went through the trigger guard, the thumb stuck out like a peg. The developer called it crooked, and he was right.
- Version 2: hands rebuilt from scratch with 3-segment fingers and fingerless gloves; the grip is solved, not eyeballed. Fingers wrap the grip cross-section, the index finger is solved onto the trigger, the thumb lies along the frame. The pistol's proportions were fixed so a real hand fits.
- Budget: arms 1504 + 844 tris, pistol 1476 tris, one 1024 hero atlas, 17-bone rig per arm.
- Made by Claude (AI) from scripts in Blender; renders are Blender Cycles, not in-engine yet.
