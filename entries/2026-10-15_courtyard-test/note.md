# First courtyard test scene

Date: 2026-10-15
Images: Blender (Cycles) renders of a test courtyard assembled from the 20 kit modules. NOT in-engine. NOT gameplay. Lighting, fog and wet floor are render-only for now; the in-engine look is next.

## What was done
- Assembled a night courtyard of the megablock from the kit only: 4 floors of facade, two levels of galleries, stairs, a bridge, neon blade signs, cables across the yard, food stall, vending machines, wrecked car.
- Two views: from the ground, and from the second-floor gallery.
- This is the visual target for the first playable area of the game.

## Who did what
- Claude (chat): built and lit the scene, rendered it.
- The boss: approved the direction ("this is fucking awesome" was the exact review).

## Caveats
- Missing for now: holographic ads, the city skyline, rain, floor decals. Those come with the Unity scene.
- Bright spots near the bottom of one render are preview-light artifacts.
